How it works

From walkthrough to spotless, in four steps.

Every client gets a written scope, a schedule that fits their hours and a crew that inspects its own work before leaving.

  1. Step 01

    Free consultation

    We walk your facility with you, note high-traffic areas, special surfaces and access needs, and listen to what has not worked before.

  2. Step 02

    A scope written for your building

    You receive a clear, written plan listing every task and how often it happens, priced for your square footage and schedule.

  3. Step 03

    Scheduled around your hours

    With 24/7 availability, cleaning happens nights, early mornings or weekends so your business never has to work around us.

  4. Step 04

    Inspected before we leave

    Crews finish with a walkthrough against your scope, and you have one point of contact for requests, add-ons and feedback.

What commercial cleaning services does Miraculous offer in Winter Haven?

Miraculous Cleaning Services LLC provides office and janitorial cleaning, commercial floor care (stripping, waxing and burnishing), pressure washing, window cleaning, post-construction cleanup and commercial deep cleaning for businesses in Winter Haven, Florida and nearby Polk County communities.

Is Miraculous available nights and weekends?

Yes. Miraculous offers 24/7 availability, so cleaning can be scheduled after hours, overnight, early morning or on weekends to avoid interrupting your business.
