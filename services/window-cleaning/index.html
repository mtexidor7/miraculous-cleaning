The short answer

What is window cleaning?

Commercial window cleaning removes dirt, water spots, pollen and fingerprints from storefront glass, office windows, glass doors and interior partitions, leaving clear, streak-free glass.

Clean glass changes how a building feels from both sides. Customers see a bright, well-kept storefront, and your team gets more natural light.

Miraculous cleans interior and exterior glass with professional squeegees and detailing, including frames, sills and tracks, on a one-time or recurring schedule.

Questions

Window Cleaning FAQ

Common questions from Winter Haven businesses about window cleaning.

How often should commercial windows be cleaned?

Retail storefronts and restaurants often clean glass weekly or every two weeks. Office buildings commonly schedule exterior windows quarterly and interior glass monthly.

Do you clean both inside and outside?

Yes. Miraculous cleans interior and exterior glass, including doors, partitions, frames and sills.

Can you remove hard water spots?

Light to moderate mineral spots can usually be removed with the right treatment. Heavy etching that has damaged the glass surface may only improve partially.
